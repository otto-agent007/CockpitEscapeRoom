Flat 1990s arcade pixel-art game backdrop layer: THIN HIGH MARTIAN DUST AND ICE CLOUDS, for the upper sky of a fighting-game stage.

COMPOSITION: wispy cloud bands spread across the ENTIRE width of the image, edge to edge. They occupy the middle 60 percent of the image height. Leave a LOT of open sky: roughly two thirds of the band must be empty. All empty sky and every gap must be absolutely OPAQUE SOLID PURE MAGENTA RGB(255,0,255). The clouds themselves must NOT be magenta.

CRITICAL — THESE ARE NOT EARTH CLOUDS. Mars has a thin dry atmosphere. Draw LONG, THIN, FLAT, HORIZONTALLY STRETCHED streaks and veils, like high cirrus or blowing dust. They must be many times wider than they are tall. Absolutely NO billowing cumulus, NO puffy rounded lobes, NO towering vertical cloud masses, NO cauliflower shapes. Vary the streaks: a few long thin ones spanning most of the width, several shorter broken wisps, and some very faint dust haze bands near the bottom.

LIGHTING: the sun is below the bottom edge, out of frame. Each streak is slightly darker along its top and catches a pale cool light along its underside.

COLOUR: exactly four flat tones and no others. Highest, faintest streaks in dusty mauve RGB(86,62,74). Mid streaks in dull rose-brown RGB(124,84,74). Lower, denser dust bands in warm tan RGB(168,124,88). The lit underside edges in pale cool grey RGB(198,186,178). Use the pale grey sparingly, as a hard 1-pixel edge only.

STYLE: flat 1990s arcade pixel art. Every tone is a hard-edged solid block with crisp stepped edges. NO gradients, NO dithering, NO blur, NO soft edges, NO glow, NO feathering, NO anti-aliasing, NO transparency, NO photographic realism, NO text.

TILING: repeated side by side horizontally forever. Content at the extreme LEFT edge must continue the content at the extreme RIGHT edge seamlessly, with no seam and no streak cut awkwardly at either edge.
