Flat 1990s arcade pixel-art game backdrop layer: BIG DRAMATIC SUNSET CLOUD BANKS lit from below, for the upper sky of a fighting-game stage.

COMPOSITION: large cloud masses spread across the ENTIRE width of the image, edge to edge. The clouds occupy the middle 60 percent of the image height. Leave generous open sky BETWEEN and AROUND the cloud masses: roughly half the band should be empty. All empty sky, and every gap between clouds, must be absolutely OPAQUE SOLID PURE MAGENTA RGB(255,0,255). The clouds themselves must NOT be magenta.

THE CLOUDS ARE THE MAIN EVENT. Make them BIG and BILLOWING, with heavy rounded lobes and soft stacked shapes, like towering sunset cumulus. Vary them strongly: two or three large dominant banks, several medium ones, and a few long thin streaks low down. They must NOT be thin horizontal lines and must NOT be a uniform even row. Overlap them so some clouds sit in front of others.

LIGHTING FROM BELOW, THIS IS THE KEY: the sun is below the bottom edge of the image and out of frame. Every cloud must be DARKEST along its TOP and BLAZING BRIGHTEST along its UNDERSIDE and its lower edges, as if backlit and underlit by a sunset. The lower clouds are hotter and brighter than the higher ones.

COLOUR: exactly four flat tones and no others. Cloud tops and shadowed masses in dark plum RGB(58,28,58). Cloud mid-bodies in deep rose RGB(107,42,68). Lit undersides in hot burnt orange RGB(194,84,47). The brightest bottom rim edges in blazing amber RGB(255,154,74). Use the amber sparingly, only as a hard-edged rim along the very lowest edge of each cloud.

STYLE: flat 1990s arcade pixel art. Every tone is a hard-edged solid block of uniform colour with crisp stepped edges, like a hand-drawn Super Nintendo sky. NO gradients, NO dithering, NO blur, NO soft edges, NO glow, NO haze, NO feathering, NO anti-aliasing, NO transparency, NO texture, NO photographic realism, NO text.

TILING: this image will be repeated side by side horizontally forever. The clouds at the extreme LEFT edge must continue the clouds at the extreme RIGHT edge seamlessly, with no seam, no gap and no cloud awkwardly cut in half at either edge.
