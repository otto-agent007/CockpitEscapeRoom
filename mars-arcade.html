How to play

Player 1: A / D move, W jumps, J light, K heavy, L special. Walk away from an incoming attack to guard. Player 2: arrow keys move and jump; comma / period / slash attack.

TEFLON DON’s Cap Throw: S, then forward + J. SCAM ALTMAN’s Non-Profit Pivot: S, then forward + K. Native Special buttons perform the same moves. Earn meter through play.

Space pauses, R restarts the round, T switches the opponent between CPU and a second player, G switches difficulty. A connected controller uses the left stick or D-pad to move, face buttons to attack, Start to pause, and Back to restart. Escape returns to your game.
