Character gym · frame

Drag a box to move it, Shift-drag to resize. Arrows nudge 1 px (Shift 5). [ ] step frames, space plays at the table's holds, O onion, M mirror, C copies the selected kind from the previous frame, Ctrl+Z undoes. The purple lines are the contract baseline (row 119) and the pivot column (64).

Size and alignment
              
Height uses nearest-pixel scaling with feet on row 119. Body alignment uses the torso, while keeping the foot center within 1px of pivot 64. Any remaining body difference is shown above; manual nudges let you compare alternatives.
