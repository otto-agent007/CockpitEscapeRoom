MARS ARCADE — BOX HARNESS

Boxes driven by the real fight loop. Tune the frame data in src/game/marsArcadeFighters.ts and save — it hot-reloads.

Solid red = hitbox live this frame. Dashed amber = startup, the telegraph. Blue outline = blocking. Faint dashed box = the 44 px sprite silhouette around the 24 px pushbox. Edge lines = the stage walls.
Reach is centre-to-centre: a move connects when the opponent's dashed centre line is inside the solid end-cap, not when the boxes overlap.
